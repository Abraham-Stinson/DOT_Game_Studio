## 📖 About the Game

**Project Mountain** is a linear FPS/Survival game themed around Pandora's Box. The player takes on the role of a lone survivor who must fight their way up a treacherous mountain, battle manifestations of Pandora's evils, and ultimately seal the Box at the summit.

## 🗺️ Game Progression

The game is divided into three phases, each with its own environment, weapons, and challenges.

### Phase 1 — The Port to the Church
The player starts at the Port and fights through scattered zombie hordes along a mountain trail, passing through a tent camp.

- **Starting weapons:** Stick, Revolver
- **Loot:** Assault Rifle (found at the Tent Camp)

### Phase 2 — The Church to the Cave
The player enters a Church and faces the **first boss**, then continues to a Wooden House and into a dark Cave.

- **Loot:** Sniper Rifle (found in the Wooden House)
- **Boss: Greed** *(inside the Church)*
  - At 50% HP, summons a zombie horde
  - At critical HP, absorbs zombies to heal itself

### Phase 3 — The Cave to Pandora's Box
A lava-filled cave leads to an ancient Feudal Settlement surrounded by stone walls — and the final boss.

- **Boss: Famine** *(inside the Feudal Settlement)*
  - Steals the player's currently equipped firearm (deal 100 damage to recover it)
  - Spawns a stationary turret enemy that fires continuously

**Win Condition:** After defeating Famine, cross a narrow bridge over a deep abyss and interact with the glowing Pandora's Box to seal it and complete the game.

## 🎨 Visual Style

Realistic visuals powered by **Unreal Engine 5**, optimized with **1K (1024×1024)** and **2K (2048×2048)** textures to balance quality and performance.

3D assets are created in **Blender** and textured with **Adobe Substance Painter**.
